# Privacy Policy

**Wild Crowns**  
Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Wild Crowns game ("the game", "we") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the rights you have over it. We process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Danish data protection law.

## The short version

- You do not sign up. The game creates an account for you on first launch, tied to your device.
- We collect what is needed to run a multiplayer game: your account, your username, your progress, your chat messages, and crash reports.
- **We show no ads. We use no advertising identifiers and no marketing trackers. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train AI models.**
- Your data is stored on servers in **Germany**, in the European Union.
- You can delete your account and everything on it from inside the game, at any time.

## 2. What data we collect

**Account data.** There is no sign-up form. The first time you open the game, it creates a player account using the identifier that your phone or tablet's operating system gives this app. That identifier is specific to Wild Crowns and is not shared with other apps. It works as your login, which is why your progress survives closing the game but is tied to the device until you link an account. You choose a username, which other players can see and which you can change in the game.

**Sign in with Apple (optional).** You can link your player account to your Apple account in Settings, under Account, so that you keep your progress if you lose or replace your phone. If you do, the sign in token from Apple is sent to our server so it can attach your Apple account to your player account. We receive the account identifier Apple gives us and, if you choose to share rather than hide it, your email address. We use these only to recognise you when you sign in again. We do not send you email. (On Android the same thing is available through Google Sign-In.)

**Game data.** Your progress, held on our server: trophies, level and experience, gold and gem balances, your card collection and card levels, your decks, battle pass progress, tower and emote unlocks, results and replays of matches you have played, your clan membership, and your friend list.

**Messages.** Clan chat messages and direct messages between friends. These are stored on our server so they can be delivered, and so that a message can be reviewed if someone reports it.

**Moderation data.** Which players you have blocked or muted, and any reports you submit. A report records the player or message you reported, the category you chose, the time, and, for a reported message, its text.

**Leaderboard data.** Your username and your trophy count appear on the public leaderboard inside the game, visible to other players.

**Crash and error reports.** The game includes Sentry, a crash reporting tool. See the next section, which describes it in full.

**Connection data.** Your device's IP address reaches our server whenever the game connects, as it must for any internet service, and appears in the server's logs. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

## 3. Crash and error reporting through Sentry

We use **Sentry** to find out when the game crashes or hits an error, so that we can fix it. This runs in every released version of the game. It is on by default and cannot currently be switched off inside the game.

**What Sentry sends when something goes wrong:**

- The crash or error itself: what failed, and where in the game's code it failed.
- Your device model, its operating system version, and the version of the game you are running.
- A random identifier generated for your installation of the game, so that repeated crashes can be recognised as coming from one player. It is not your name, your email, or your player account, and we cannot use it to look you up in the game.
- The game's recent log output, which is attached to the report automatically. This is technical text the game writes about what it was doing.

**What Sentry does not send.** We have not enabled Sentry's option to include personally identifying information, so reports do not carry your IP address or your account details. Sentry is not used for analytics, for measuring what players do, or for advertising.

**Where it goes.** Sentry receives this data on servers in the **European Union**, and acts as our data processor. Reports are deleted automatically after at most 90 days.

## 4. What we do not collect or do

- No advertising identifiers (IDFA or AAID), no ad networks, and no ads in the game.
- No third party analytics or marketing trackers.
- No tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites.
- No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.
- No location data of any kind. The game does not request or use your location.
- **No in-app purchases in this version.** The game shows gems and gold, but they cannot be bought with real money in this release, and we take no payment details. If we enable purchases in a future version, we will update this policy first.
- We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.
- We do not use your data to train AI or machine learning models.

## 6. Who can see or receive your data

**Other players.** Your username, your trophy count, your level, and your clan are visible to other players, in matches, in clans, and on the leaderboard. That is how the game works. Chat messages are visible to the people in that chat: a clan chat is visible to the clan, a direct message to the friend you sent it to.

**You control who can message you.** In Settings you can set who may send you direct messages: everyone, friends only, or nobody. You can also block or mute individual players.

**Service providers (data processors).** They process data only on our instructions:

| Provider | What they do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Hetzner Online GmbH** | Hosting for the game server and its database, where your account, progress and messages live | Germany |
| **Sentry** | Crash and error reporting | European Union |
| **Apple / Google** | Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In on Android, if you choose to link it | Per their own terms |

**Authorities.** We disclose data only where the law requires it.

## 7. International transfers

Your data is stored and processed **in the European Union**. The game server and its database are in Germany, and Sentry receives crash reports in the EU. We do not transfer your data outside the EU. If you link Sign in with Apple, Apple handles that sign in under its own privacy policy.

## 8. How long we keep your data

- **Your account, progress, messages, clan membership and friend list:** until you delete your account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. See [Delete Your Account](./delete-account.html).
- **Server backups:** the database is backed up so it can be restored after a failure. Backups are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted, so data you have deleted may still exist in a backup for that period.
- **Crash reports at Sentry:** deleted automatically after at most 90 days.
- **Reports about other players:** kept while they are needed for moderation, so that repeat behaviour can be recognised. A report you made about another player is about that player, and it is not deleted when you delete your own account.

## 9. Security

- All traffic between the game and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS and TLS).
- The server is access controlled, and the database is not reachable from the public internet.
- Your account, progress, currency and trophies are stored and updated on our server rather than on your device.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim this one is.

**Something we want to be clear about:** chat in Wild Crowns is **not end to end encrypted**. Messages are stored on our server, protected by the measures above, and we can read them in narrow circumstances, in particular when a message is reported and has to be reviewed, or where the law requires it. Please do not share sensitive personal information in chat.

**If a data breach happens:** once we become aware of it, we will notify the Danish Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) within 72 hours, as Art. 33 GDPR requires, and tell affected players where the breach is likely to put them at high risk.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Rectify** inaccurate data (you can change your username in the game);
- **Erase** your data (delete your account in the game, or email us);
- **Restrict** or **object to** certain processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
- **Data portability**: receive your data in a machine readable format;
- **Withdraw consent** where processing is based on it, without affecting processing that already happened.

To exercise any of these rights, email **gomerd.haman12@gmail.com**. We answer within one month. Because accounts are created from a device identifier rather than an email address, please send your request from the device you play on where possible, and include your in-game username, so we can find the right account and do not hand your data to somebody else.

You also have the right to complain to the Danish Data Protection Authority:
**Datatilsynet**, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark, [www.datatilsynet.dk](https://www.datatilsynet.dk)

## 11. Children

Wild Crowns is rated for players aged **13 and over**, and in some countries the App Store rating is higher. The game has chat between players, which is why it is not aimed at younger children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below the applicable age. If you believe a child below it is playing, contact us and we will delete the account.

Parents can restrict downloads using Screen Time on iPhone and iPad, or Family Link and Google Play parental controls on Android.

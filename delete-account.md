# Delete Your Account

You can delete your Wild Crowns account and everything on it at any time, from inside the game. Deletion is **immediate and permanent**, and it cannot be undone. There is no recovery, no grace period, and no way for us to restore your progress afterwards.

## Option 1: In the game (recommended, instant)

1. Open **Wild Crowns**
2. Tap the **gear icon** on the main menu to open **Settings**
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **DELETE ACCOUNT**
4. Confirm twice. The second dialog is headed **FINAL WARNING**, and the button is **DELETE FOREVER**

The game deletes your account on the server straight away and then restarts itself. When it opens again it is a fresh install with no progress.

## Option 2: By email

If you cannot reach Settings, or you have already deleted the game from your device, send an email to **gomerd.haman12@gmail.com** with the subject **"Account deletion request"** and your in-game username.

Accounts are created from a device identifier rather than an email address, so we have no email address on file to check a request against, and we cannot delete an account on the word of an email alone. To let us confirm the account is yours, send the request **from the device you play on** where that is possible, or reinstall the game and use Option 1, which is faster and does not require us to verify anything. Where we can identify the account, we delete it and confirm to you within 30 days, as required by the GDPR.

## What gets deleted

Everything on the account:

- The account itself and its login
- Your username, level, experience, and trophies
- Your gold and gem balances
- Your card collection, card levels, and decks
- Your battle pass progress, and your tower and emote unlocks
- Your match history and saved replays
- Your friend list, your clan membership, and your blocks and mutes
- Your entry on the trophy leaderboard
- Your local save data on the device

Your Apple account link, if you made one, is removed with the account. Deleting your player account does not affect your Apple account itself.

## What is not deleted straight away

- **Database backups.** The game server is backed up so it can be restored after a failure. Backups are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted, so your data may still exist in a backup during that period. It is not used for anything else.
- **Crash reports.** Reports sent to Sentry when the game crashed are deleted automatically after at most 90 days. They are not attached to your player account and cannot be traced back to it.
- **Messages you already sent.** A message you sent to a clan chat or to a friend has already been delivered to them. It may still appear in their chat history.
- **Reports you filed about other players.** A report you submitted is about someone else's behaviour and is kept while it is needed for moderation.

For the full picture, see the [Privacy Policy](./privacy.html).

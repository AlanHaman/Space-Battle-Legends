# Space Battle Legends

Space Battle Legends is a real time multiplayer battler for iPhone and iPad. Build a deck, play units and spells against another player, and climb the trophy ladder.

This site holds the support and legal information for the game:

- **[Privacy Policy](./privacy.html)**: what data the game collects, why, and your rights
- **[Terms of Service](./terms.html)**: the rules for playing
- **[Delete Your Account](./delete-account.html)**: how to delete your account and its data

## Common questions

**I changed phone and my progress is gone.**
Your account is tied to the device it was created on. To carry it to a new phone, link it before you switch: **Settings**, then **Account**, then **Sign in with Apple**. Once it is linked, sign in with the same Apple account on the new device and your progress comes with it. If you did not link it and the old device is gone, we usually cannot recover the account, because there is nothing on it that identifies you to us.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the game: **Settings**, then **DELETE ACCOUNT** at the bottom. Full instructions, and what is deleted, are on the [Delete Your Account](./delete-account.html) page.

**Someone is being abusive in chat.**
Tap the player or the message to **report** it, and choose a category. You can also **block** them, which stops their messages reaching you, or **mute** them. We review reports and act on objectionable content within 24 hours.

**I do not want strangers messaging me.**
In **Settings** you can set who may send you direct messages: everyone, friends only, or nobody.

**Are there in-app purchases?**
No. This version has no purchases of any kind. Gems and gold are earned by playing and cannot be bought with real money.

**How do I report a bug?**
Email us with your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot or a short screen recording helps a great deal, especially for anything visual or anything that happened mid match.

**The game will not connect.**
Space Battle Legends needs an internet connection for every match, because matches run on our server. If it will not connect, check your connection first, then try again in a few minutes in case the server is being updated. If it keeps happening, email us and say roughly when it started.
